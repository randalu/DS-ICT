## Known issues

1. **`assets/uploads/` is empty locally.** Every `media/*` and uploads image 404s;
   heroes fall back to `assets/images/galle-lighthouse.jpg`. Destination and
   experience galleries cannot be judged until production uploads are restored.
2. **Pushing can fail with `Could not resolve host: github.com`.** Transient;
   the identical retry succeeds. Do not force-push or rewrite history to "fix" it.
3. **`InventoryTriageTest::testExperienceTemplateEmitsItsExistingSchemaGraph`
   was rewritten** (commit `67bd27b`) to assert the guarantee in
   `views/v3/layout.php` instead of the old `experience.php` source. The
   behaviour it protects (schema emitted before the footer) is unchanged.
4. **Legal policy pages are unpublished**, so the footer legal links 404. Content
   gate, not a routing bug.
5. **OpenRouter key still needs rotating** at the provider (removed from
   `site_settings` in the Phase 0 migration).

## Rules that have held all the way through

- No URL, slug, sitemap or redirect changes.
- `includes/seo.php` and `schema_output()` remain the only metadata emitters.
- No database schema changes.
- Every family keeps its existing JSON-LD type (`schema_trip`,
  `schema_destination`, `schema_experience`, `schema_service`,
  `schema_article`, `schema_item_list`).
- Unknown or unpublished slugs return 404, never a soft 200.
